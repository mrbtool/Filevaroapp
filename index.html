About Smart Image Tools

Smart Image Tools (developed by mrbushx) is your ultimate all-in-one suite designed to make daily image editing tasks quick, secure, and incredibly easy.

Unlike other applications that upload your personal files to the cloud, Smart Image Tools was built with a strict 100% Offline architecture. It uses the hardware capabilities of your own device to process everything instantly, saving your data and protecting your privacy.

Core Features:

  • Image Resizer: Resize images easily for social media, websites, or email attachments.
  • ID Photo Cropper: Crop ID photos perfectly to professional passport and visa dimensions.
  • Increase/Reduce Image Size: Modify the file size (KB/MB) effortlessly while maintaining visual quality.
  • Universal Image Converter: Convert formats instantly between JPG, PNG, and WebP.
  • Join Images & PDF Tools: Merge images together or convert PDF pages directly into high-quality images.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our absolute highest priority. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict data handling practices for Smart Image Tools.

1. 100% Offline Processing

Smart Image Tools is designed to work completely offline. Your photos, documents, and files never leave your device. We do not have servers that collect, process, or store your images.

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not require an account, email address, or login to use the app.

3. Permissions

The app only requests permission to read and write to your device's storage. This is strictly required so the app can select the images you want to edit and save the finished result to your Gallery (in the 'ImageResizer' folder).

4. Third-Party Analytics

Because the app is completely offline and ad-free, we do not utilize third-party trackers or data brokers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Where are my edited images saved?

A: All processed files are saved automatically directly to your phone's Gallery, inside a dedicated folder named 'ImageResizer'.

Q: Do I need an internet connection to use the app?

A: No. Smart Image Tools is entirely self-contained and works 100% offline. You can edit images on airplane mode or without an active data plan.

Q: Are there any hidden fees or watermarks?

A: No! The app features No Ads, No Logins, and No Watermarks on your processed files.

Q: Can I change the default save format?

A: Yes! Open the app, go to Settings > Default Image Format, and you can choose whether files are saved as JPG, PNG, or WebP by default.

Q: How do I clear my recent history?

A: Simply go to Settings and click "Clear Recent Files". This will instantly wipe the history shown on your Recent tab.
